Choosing the number of clusters
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Stability = adjusted Rand index between 10 independent refits on
  random 80% subsets of the genes. It measures whether the same genes
  keep landing together, which is the property we actually need.

  Two rules, both fixed in advance, because they answer different
  questions:
    most separated       highest silhouette among k with stability
                         above 0.75. Answers 'what is the single
                         cleanest split'. Beware: silhouette
                         structurally favours small k, so on
                         continuous data it usually lands on k=2.
    most granular stable largest k whose stability still reaches 0.95.
                         Answers 'how much structure can we resolve
                         and still reproduce'. This is the default,
                         because the task is to describe expression
                         patterns rather than find one dichotomy.
