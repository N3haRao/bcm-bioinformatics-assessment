Gene selection for the sample distances
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A gene that does not move between samples contributes only noise to
  the distance between them, so the distances are computed on the most
  variable genes. The selection is blind to any sample grouping, it
  only asks whether a gene varies at all.

  most variable genes used : 2,000 of 9,767
  log2 variance cut-off    : 0.2296
  median log2 variance, selected vs all : 0.5891 vs 0.0642

Why the raw correlation matrix is not enough
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  all genes, not gene-centred    : off-diagonal r from 0.8747 to 0.9893  (range 0.1146)
  variable genes, gene-centred   : off-diagonal r from -0.8190 to 0.7863  (range 1.6053)
  The second spreads the samples over a 14.0x wider range, which is
  the difference between resolving the groups and not.
